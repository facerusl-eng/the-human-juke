## Body
The Human Jukebox launches a new live music experience where audiences can request songs and vote favorites up in a live queue directly from their phones.

The platform is built for artists, hosts, and venues that want stronger engagement during live performances without losing control over show quality and flow.

It combines a mobile-first audience experience with a host control panel that manages event status, setlists, and moderation in real time. The result is a more social and dynamic show where audience members become active participants instead of passive listeners.
